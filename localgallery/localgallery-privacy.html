Privacy Policy for Gallery

Last updated: 10 Nov 2026

Gallery ("the app") is an Android app, developed by Nuko ("we", "our"), for viewing the photos and videos on your phone in galleries made from the folders you choose. This page explains what data the app handles — and what it does not collect.

Short version

Your photos and videos are handled only on your phone; they are never sent to us (we do not run any server). The index, galleries, favorites and tags are stored only on your device. When you open the map, map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap. The free version shows ads, so Google's ad SDK (AdMob) handles data such as the advertising ID (details below). The Pro version (one-time purchase) shows no ads.

How the app uses your photos and videos

  • Reading: through Android's MediaStore, the app reads the list of photos and videos on your device (folders, file names, sizes, dates and so on) and their thumbnails and contents. If you allow access to selected photos only (Android 14 and later), the app handles only the ones you allowed.
  • Writing: the app changes files only when you choose to copy, move or delete them. Moves and deletions happen only after you allow them in Android's own confirmation dialog (deleted items go to Android's trash).
  • Sharing: only when you tap Share, the photos and videos you selected are handed to the app you pick.
  • New-folder detection: when photos or videos are added or moved, the app updates its list on the device and tells you about new folders (with a notification while the app is closed). All of this happens on your device.

The map and photo locations

  • Photo locations: the place a photo or video was taken (location metadata such as EXIF) is read automatically on the device for the photos in your galleries, and is kept only in the index on your device. It is never sent to us. The app never uses your device's current location.
  • Map images: when you open the map, images for the area on screen are loaded from OpenStreetMap's tile servers (tile.openstreetmap.org), so your IP address and the area you are viewing reach the OpenStreetMap Foundation. Your photos and the list of their locations are not sent. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy.

What is stored on your device

  • Index: folder names, file names, sizes and dates of your photos and videos, plus photo dimensions and locations, video lengths and playback positions. It is used to show lists quickly.
  • What you add: galleries and their folders, which galleries are hidden, favorites, tags, smart albums and which albums are private.
  • This data is excluded from Android's cloud backup and is deleted when you uninstall the app (your photos and videos themselves are not deleted).

Hidden galleries and the app lock

Showing a hidden gallery or the All tab again, the app lock (Pro) and showing private albums use your device's own screen lock (fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern) through Android's standard BiometricPrompt. The app never receives or stores a password or biometric data.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows a small banner ad from Google AdMob at the bottom of list screens (never while a photo or video is shown). AdMob may collect and use the advertising ID, device information and approximate location (inferred from the IP address) to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Users in the EEA, the UK and similar regions are asked for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform, and can change it at any time from "Ad privacy options" in Settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings.

In-app purchase

Purchasing Pro is handled entirely by Google Play. Payment details never reach us.

Permissions

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED: to list and show the photos and videos on your device. This is the app's core feature.
  • ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION: to show where photos were taken on the map. The device's current location is not used.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: to tell you about new folders (can be turned off in Settings).
  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: for map images, ads and the in-app purchase. Never used to send your photos or videos.
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: to boost the volume above 100% in the built-in player.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC: to check your screen lock when showing hidden galleries again and for the app lock.
  • AD_ID: for ads in the free version (see "Advertising" above).
  • The app has no current-location, camera, contacts or all-files-access permission.

What we do not collect

  • Your photos and videos, their names, contents or locations
  • The names or layout of your galleries and folders
  • Personal information such as your name or e-mail
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to us

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age in your country) and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
