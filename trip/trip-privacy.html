Privacy Policy for Trips

Last updated: 13 Oct 2026

Trips ("the app") is a travel log for trips abroad on Android, developed by Nuko ("we", "our"). This page explains what data the app handles — and what it does not collect.

Short version

Everything you add — trips, flights and other legs, the countries you visited, and the documents you import (booking e-mails, PDFs, screenshots) — is stored only on your device and is never sent to us (we do not run any server). The free version shows ads, so Google's ad SDK (AdMob) handles data such as the advertising ID (details below). The Pro version (one-time purchase) shows no ads.

What stays on your device

  • Imported documents: only the booking e-mails, PDFs, images and text you share to the app or pick are copied into the app's private storage.
  • Reading flights (OCR): reading text from screenshots and PDFs is done on your device with Google ML Kit. Images and text are never sent to a server (the recognition model is downloaded through Google Play services). Finding flight numbers, airports and times is also done on the device.
  • Airport data: the table used to look up countries and time zones from airport codes is bundled with the app, so looking up an airport never goes online.
  • Backups: backup files (ZIP) are written only to the location you choose.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows a small banner ad from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the advertising ID, device information and approximate location (inferred from the IP address) to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Users in the EEA, the UK and similar regions are asked for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform, and can change it at any time from "Ad privacy options" in the app menu. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings.

In-app purchase

Purchasing Pro is handled entirely by Google Play. Payment details never reach us.

Permissions

  • INTERNET: for ads and the in-app purchase. It is never used to send your trips or documents.
  • The app has no camera, location, contacts or e-mail account permission. It only sees the e-mails you share from your mail app or save as .eml files.

What we do not collect

  • Personal information such as your name, e-mail or passport details
  • Your trips, flights, visited countries or imported documents
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to us

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age in your country) and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
