Privacy Policy for AudioSync

Last updated: 13 Nov 2026

AudioSync ("the app") is an Android app, developed by Nuko ("we", "our"), that fixes videos whose sound is out of sync with the picture. This page explains what data the app handles — and what it does not collect.

Short version

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data through AudioSync. Playing videos, drawing their waveforms and exporting fixed copies all happen on your device — your videos are never uploaded to any server. However, the Free version shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google (as the ad provider) may obtain your advertising ID, IP address, and device information to deliver ads, prevent fraud, and measure performance. The Pro version shows no ads and makes no such connections. We do not use any analytics tools.

What the app does with your videos

AudioSync reads only the video you choose in the app, or one handed to it by another app through "Share" or "Open with". It plays that video and analyses its audio on the device to draw the waveform. It does not read any other photos or videos on your phone.

When you tap "Export", a copy of the video with the audio shifted is saved to the "Movies/AudioSync" folder on your device. The original video is not changed. If the original video contains orientation and location tags, they are carried over to the copy as they are (the app itself never uses or sends location data). Temporary files used during export are created in the app's private storage and deleted when the export finishes.

The only settings the app keeps are small ones such as a cached purchase state. They are stored only in the app's private storage and are deleted when you uninstall the app. They are not sent off the device by Android's backup either (backup is disabled for the app).

Advertising

The Free version shows a small Google AdMob banner at the bottom of the screen. Ads are served by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, and Google may obtain your Advertising ID, IP address, and device and app information to deliver ads, prevent fraud, and measure performance. This information is handled by Google and is not sent to us. For details, see Google's policies.

In the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and other regions where it is required, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) is shown so you can choose your ad preferences. You can change your choice at any time from "Ad privacy options" in the app's menu. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings.

Buying the Pro version removes all ads. The app is not directed at children under 13, and ads are not configured as child-directed.

Permissions

AudioSync does not request storage, photos/videos, location, contacts, camera, or microphone permissions. It reads only the videos you pick or share with it, through Android's own picker and sharing. The permissions it declares are listed below.

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: used only to show ads and to check purchases with Google Play. They are never used to send videos anywhere.
  • WAKE_LOCK / FOREGROUND_SERVICE: declared by libraries such as the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The app itself does not run a foreground service.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID / ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID / ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION / ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS: declared by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for ad delivery and measurement in the Free version.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: used for the Pro in-app purchase through Google Play. The purchase itself is handled by Google Play, and payment details never reach us.

Data collection and analytics

We do not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, location, etc.)
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to any server of ours (we do not run any servers)
  • Your videos, their audio, or their file names (never transmitted off your device)

Note that, as described under "Advertising" above, in the Free version Google may obtain information in order to serve ads. This is not collection by us.

Third-party services

The third-party services AudioSync integrates are Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform) for ads, and Google's Play Billing Library for in-app purchases. The Android Media3 libraries used to play and export videos run entirely on the device and make no network connections. The app does not include any analytics platform or cloud storage service. For how Google handles information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy
