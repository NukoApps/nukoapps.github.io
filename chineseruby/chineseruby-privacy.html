Privacy Policy for Chinese Ruby

Last updated: 25 Nov 2026

Chinese Ruby ("the app") is an Android app, developed by Nuko ("we", "our"), that adds zhuyin and pinyin to Chinese text. This page explains what data the app does — and does not — handle.

Short version

Adding readings to your text, displaying it and reading it aloud all happen on your device. We do not operate any server and never collect or store your personal data or the text you enter. Network communication happens in only four situations:

  • Online mode (optional): when you choose "Online", the app downloads the pinyin-pro reading library from a CDN (jsDelivr) and runs it on your device. Only the request for the script goes out; the text you enter is never sent. "Offline" mode makes no network requests at all.
  • AI check (optional): only when you have entered your own Gemini API key in Settings and press the ✨ button, the text on screen is sent directly from your device to Google's Gemini API, using your key, and the reading of each character is sent back. We never receive or store this text ourselves.
  • Ads (Free version only): the Free version shows a banner ad at the top of the screen using Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address, device information, and information about ad impressions and interactions. The Pro version shows no ads and makes no ad-related network requests.
  • Pro purchase: buying Pro (which removes the ads) is handled through Google Play Billing. Your payment details (such as card numbers) are processed by Google Play and never shared with us. The app only retrieves whether you have purchased Pro from Google Play.

About ads (Free version)

Ads in the Free version are served by Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID and similar data to show interest-based (personalized) ads. In the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and other applicable regions, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) is shown on first launch, and you can change your choice at any time from "Ad privacy options" in the app's Settings. You can also delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Android's settings. For how Google handles data, see Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Reading aloud

Reading aloud uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. The text being read is passed to that engine; if you use an engine with online voices, its provider's policy applies.

What's stored on your device

The text you enter, your display and voice settings, your Pro purchase status, and any Gemini API key you enter are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. We never receive this data. The "Clear" button removes the text, and uninstalling the app removes everything.

Permissions

  • Internet (INTERNET) and network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): used only to download the script in Online mode, for the optional AI check, for ads in the Free version, and to check your Pro purchase. Offline mode adds readings without any network access.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): used by Google AdMob to serve ads in the Free version. You can delete or reset your advertising ID at any time in Android's settings.

What we do not collect

We operate no server of our own, and we do not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, location, etc.)
  • The text you enter or its readings
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to any server of ours

Third-party services

The app communicates with the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • jsDelivr (serves the pinyin-pro script in Online mode; no text is sent)
  • Google Gemini API (only when you use the AI check, with your own API key, directly from your device)
  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (ads in the Free version, and consent collection in applicable regions)
  • Google Play Billing (processing the Pro purchase)

No other analytics platform or ad network is included.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the relevant age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
