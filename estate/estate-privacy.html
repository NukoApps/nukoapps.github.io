Privacy Policy for Landlord Note

Last updated: 3 Nov 2026

Landlord Note (大家ノート, "the app") is a management app for owners of rental properties such as investment condos, for Android and Windows, developed by Nuko ("we", "our"). This page explains what data the app handles — and what it does not collect.

Short version

Everything you add — properties, loans, tenants, your ledger of rent and expenses, and the documents you import (e-mails from your management company, repayment schedules, receipts) — is stored only on your device and is never sent to us (we do not run any server). The free Android version shows ads, so Google's ad SDK (AdMob) handles data such as the advertising ID (details below). The Pro version (one-time purchase) shows no ads.

What stays on your device

  • Properties, loans and ledger: property details, loan terms and balances, tenancy records (the tenant's name is optional), rent and expense transactions and tax estimates are kept in a database in the app's private storage. They are also excluded from Google's automatic backup.
  • Imported documents: only the e-mails, PDFs, images and text you share to the app, pick or photograph are copied into the app's private storage. Photos are taken by your device's camera app; the app itself has no camera permission.
  • Reading text (OCR): on Android, reading text from receipts and repayment schedules is done on your device with Google ML Kit. Images and text are never sent to a server (the models for Japanese, Korean, Chinese and other scripts are downloaded through Google Play services). Finding amounts and dates and comparing repayment schedules is also done on the device. The Windows version reads the text layer of PDFs on your PC.
  • Backups and CSV: backup files (ZIP) and CSV exports are written only to the location you choose.

Exchange rates

If you have properties in a foreign currency, the app fetches European Central Bank reference rates from a public API (api.frankfurter.dev) only when you tap the fetch button under Settings > Exchange rates. Only currency codes (e.g. USD, JPY) and the date range are sent — never your properties or amounts. You can also enter rates by hand.

Advertising (free Android version)

The free version shows a small banner ad from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the advertising ID, device information and approximate location (inferred from the IP address) to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Users in the EEA, the UK and similar regions are asked for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform, and can change it at any time from "Ad privacy settings" in Settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings. Ads are never based on your properties or ledger.

In-app purchase

Purchasing Pro is handled entirely by Google Play. Payment details never reach us.

Permissions

  • INTERNET: for ads, the in-app purchase and fetching exchange rates. It is never used to send your properties, ledger or documents.
  • The app has no camera, location, contacts, e-mail account or bank account access. It only sees the e-mails and bank documents you share from other apps or pick as files.

What we do not collect

  • Personal information such as your name, e-mail or address
  • Your properties, loans, tenants, income and expenses, tax estimates or documents
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to us

About tax figures

Tax, depreciation, sale-gain and yield figures in the app are estimates based on general rules. Their accuracy is not guaranteed, and we accept no liability for any loss arising from filings, tax payments, investment decisions or anything else based on them. Check with your tax authority or a qualified professional and file at your own responsibility.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age in your country) and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
